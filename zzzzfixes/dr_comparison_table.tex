  & Not applicable

  & \xmark \\

No ball-arrest reward
  & No reward for ball having low absolute velocity after save
  & Not applicable
  & Same gap: \texttt{hand\_proximity\_strict} rewards hand-near-ball, not ball stopping. Add $\exp(-k|v_y|)\cdot\mathbf{1}[\text{saved}]$ to incentivise true arrest
  & \xmark \\

AMP motion prior
  & 6$\times$ AMP discriminators, 40\,\% reward share
  & Not applicable
  & Not implemented; explicit motion tracking used instead ($\approx$17 pts total vs 25 pts task)
  & \xmark \\

HIM-PPO
  & History encoder + ball/region estimators in actor
  & Not applicable
  & Not implemented; standard PPO; full ball obs given to actor
